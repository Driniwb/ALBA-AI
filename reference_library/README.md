# ALBA AI 3.1 – Reference Library

This public repository contains **short, original editorial summaries** of published Albanian legislative drafting, reporting-style and selected linguistic research guidance. It does not reproduce the full manuals. Each entry links to an identifiable source and location. The summaries are **editorial drafts**, not legally binding provisions, official quotations, or expert-approved terminology. The glossary contains **candidate translations**, not mandatory equivalents.

## Sources

1. Ministry of Justice / JUSTAL, *Manual per Hartimin e Legjislacionit*, 3rd edition (2023): https://justal.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Manual-per-hartimin-e-legjislacionit_Online.pdf
2. Ministry of Justice / JUSTAL, *Udhezues per Stilin e Raportimit* (February 2024): https://www.drejtesia.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Udhezues-per-stilin-e-raportimit_Online.pdf

Page numbers in `rules.csv` are **printed manual pages**, not necessarily PDF viewer page indices. Check the source publication before treating a summary as authoritative. Do not automatically replace legal terminology: context and relevant legislation control. The language assistant must not infer a legal position or institutional approval from a stylistic rule.

3. Albanian Academy of Sciences / Institute of Linguistics and Literature, *Drejtshkrimi i gjuhës shqipe* (1973), bibliographic record: https://search.worldcat.org/title/Drejtshkrimi-i-gjuhes-shqipe/oclc/247236720 . A 178-page partial scan was supplied for internal verification. The source is **historical** and contains spelling rules, **not an orthographic dictionary**. PDF text extraction contains substantial character-recognition errors. Only carefully paraphrased rules are included; the scan itself is **not distributed**. Current orthographic usage must be checked against more recent authoritative resources when uncertain.

These are **research or opinion pieces, not official binding orthographic or legal norms**. Their vocabulary and examples may be historical or contested. In particular, no mass replacement from borrowed words to suggested Albanian forms is authorised. A proposed equivalent must preserve legal and technical meaning in context. The full user-supplied PDFs were **not** committed to the public repository. Full screening decisions for all 20 materials are in `material_review_2026-11.md`.

## Second screening: 12 uploaded PDFs (9 November 2026)

Four sources were integrated in a restricted form: the Council of Europe *Glossary of the European Convention on Human Rights* (2016; selected terminology candidates only); Agim Spahiu, *Gabimet më të shpeshta gjuhësore në shtypin tonë* (2002; revised 2007); *Fjalët jostandarde në FGJSSH* (2008); and *Eufemizmat, ironia dhe fjalët me kuptim keqësues* (2008). Only seven short paraphrases (R31-R37) and ten candidate bilingual terms were added; **no full PDF, extensive wordlist, or source prose was copied to GitHub**.

The Council of Europe glossary is an institutional terminology resource for ECHR law, **not an authoritative general Albanian dictionary**; it is copyrighted (© Council of Europe 2016, reproduction permission reserved), and some translations contain editorial errors. Its entries remain candidate terms pending expert checking. Linguistic studies are descriptive, historical or author opinions and are not binding orthographic rules. The eight other PDFs are classified in `material_review_2026-11_batch2.md` and were not activated.

## Files

- `sources.csv`: bibliographic metadata and review status.
- `rules.csv`: paraphrased writing principles, topic keywords and source pointers.
- `glossary.csv`: proposed bilingual expressions for expert review.
- `documents/`: placeholder for public documents whose redistribution is authorised; **no full manuals are reproduced here**.

## Review process

Before marking entries expert-approved, check their exact scope and source passage, spelling, interpretation, and context. Maintain a reviewer name/role, review date, and revision history when approving changes. Do not upload internal GIZ materials, personal data, drafts, protected partner documents, or access credentials. This repository is public.
