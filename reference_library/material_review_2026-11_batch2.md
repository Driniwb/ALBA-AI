# ALBA AI – Screening of 12 additional Albanian PDFs (9 November 2026)

Scope: usefulness for Albanian institutional and legal drafting. This evaluation is not peer review of the studies. **Only original, limited editorial summaries and small candidate terminology samples are committed; none of the source PDFs is redistributed.**

## Selected for limited reference-library integration (4)

| File | Publication | Added content | Risk and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Glossary_sqi_web_BIL.pdf` | Council of Europe, *Glossary of the European Convention on Human Rights*, 2016 (50 pp.) | Source COE-ECHR-2016; rule R37; 10 context-labelled candidate translations | High institutional provenance; ECHR-specific, copyrighted. Some entries show spelling/translation imperfections (e.g. 'gjykatësë'). No wholesale import, no assertions of universal legal equivalence. The publisher states that reproduction permission is required. |
| `gabime_me_te_shpeshta_ne_shtypin_tone.pdf` | Agim Spahiu, *Gabimet më të shpeshta gjuhësore në shtypin tonë*, 2002, revised 2007 (13 pp.) | Rules R31-R34: verb mood/person, participles, compounds, connective constructions | Practical examples; still an older linguistic article, not the current official normative reference. Certain author proposals, including debates about standardisation, deliberately excluded. |
| `fjalet_jostandarde_ne_fgjssh.pdf` | Agim Spahiu, *Fjalët jostandarde në FGJSSH*, 2008 (18 pp.) | Rule R35: register awareness | Source describes labels of a **1980** dictionary; no wholesale exclusion of dialectal/historical or potentially technical terms. Relevance to institutional style is an editorial inference. |
| `eufemizmat_ne_fgjssh.pdf` | Agim Spahiu, *Eufemizmat, ironia dhe fjalët me kuptim keqësues*, 2008 (85 pp.) | Rule R36: neutral, accurate official tone | Dictionary categories illustrate pragmatic nuance; avoids classifying all figurative expressions as errors. Neutral-tone guidance is editorial, not a directly promulgated rule. |

## Useful background but not active editorial rules (3)

| File | Assessment |
|---|---|
| `homonimia_ne_fgjssh.pdf` | Extensive catalogue of homonyms from FGJSSH (1980); contextual meaning already covered by existing ALBA precision/terminology guidance; not safe as direct automatic substitutions. |
| `antonimet_ne_fgjsh.pdf` | Historical list of antonyms from FGJSSH (1980). Contrasting terms is a semantic topic, not a standard-writing rule, and some oppositions are context-dependent. |
| `hakiu_kundervenia.pdf` | Research on r/rr phonological opposition and its written effects; useful for study and testing, but a small older sociophonetic survey, not an authoritative spelling dictionary. |

## Not useful for the current ALBA drafting use case (5)

| File | Reason |
|---|---|
| `fjalet_emertuese_ne_fgjssh.pdf` | Statistical counts of parts of speech in FGJSSH (1980); no direct normative drafting recommendation. |

## Rules for safe use

1. The Council of Europe resource applies in the ECHR/human-rights domain; match wording to applicable Albanian law when used elsewhere.
2. All sample terms have status `candidate` and all new editorial rules status `editorial_draft`; sources remain `pending` expert approval.
3. Do not copy whole published PDF files or bulk extract reference lexicons into a public GitHub repository. In particular, the CoE PDF contains an explicit reproduction notice.
4. Verify morphology and case agreement against an authoritative contemporary Albanian grammar before presenting suggestions as definite normative corrections.
5. Current code retrieves short paraphrases and candidates based on keyword matching; passing GitHub static tests is **not** evidence of output quality.
